## Root cause found (before any fix)

**The ring math is correct.** `strokeDashoffset = circumference × progress`, so the visible arc is exactly `(1 − progress)` — at "8" of 20 it draws 40%. The defect is contrast: the track is `rgba(255,255,255,0.14)`, which composites to ≈`#2c443f` over the `#0a2620` background — **1.5:1**, below the 3:1 WCAG minimum for a graphical object. You cannot see where the arc *ends*, so a 40% arc reads as "almost full". Your complaints #1 and #4 are one defect, and I'll fix the cause rather than the symptom.

Also found: `.takeover__progress` / `.takeover__pip` are dead CSS (no JSX uses them) — free to repurpose for the linear bar.
